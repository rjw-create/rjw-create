# Hey, I'm Jackson Raines 👋

High school student in Lexington, KY, interested in electrical/computer engineering, computer science, and AI. I like building things that sit at the intersection of hardware and software, from embedded sensor systems to the math behind scheduling algorithms.

## What I'm Working On

**🔬 Subsurface Imaging Toolkit** — Designed and built a handheld geophysical sensor that fuses Electrical Resistivity Tomography and 24 GHz FMCW Radar for locating buried objects. Arduino firmware, 16-bit ADC acquisition, custom signal processing pipeline, and a 3D-printed enclosure. [[Repo]](https://github.com/rjw-create/ert-fmcw-radar) [[Project Page]](https://rjw-create.github.io/ert-fmcw-radar/)

**📐 PAES-S — Staleness-Aware Edge AI Scheduling** — Research contributor on the theoretical analysis of a priority-aware scheduler for multi-model edge AI systems. Authored the mathematical section: monotonicity proofs, a tier-dominance impossibility result, and a bounded-normalization design recommendation. Manuscript in preparation.
